EXP-003: one learner per variant, every variant stays relevant negative (-0.3886 to -0.2188).
- **Model variants and grid (preregistered).** Manuscript sections 5.2 and 5.3.
- **M4 sensitivity (exploratory, J-0018).** Manuscript section 5.6 and `results/exploratory/M4-SENSITIVITY/summary.csv`.
